A hypothetical device proposed by [[Vannevar Bush]] in 1945: a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents, where the connections between documents are as valuable as the documents themselves.

## Relation to the LLM Wiki Pattern

[[Andrej Karpathy]] frames the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] as related in spirit to the Memex — both are private, actively curated stores rather than open/public systems like the web became. The part Bush's vision left unsolved was *who does the maintenance* (updating cross-references, keeping synthesis current). The LLM Wiki Pattern's core claim is that an LLM can now do that maintenance at near-zero cost.
